O1|S5,S6,S10,S11|Ethanol and lactate illustrate fermentation use by being products of fermentation processes, as ethanol is used in alcoholic beverages and lactate is used for food preservation and flavoring. The usual pasteurization temperature is less than 100 °C (212 °F), which is mild enough to avoid significant changes to the nutritional and sensory qualities of the food, contrasting with the higher temperatures used in fermentation processes.
