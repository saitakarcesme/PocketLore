Changes: FineWiki extracted structured text from Wikimedia Enterprise HTML.
PocketLore compresses text and original wikitext, builds a lexical index,
and selects an explicitly labeled lead for lower-priority articles.
Original wikitext is preserved in the pinned source archive. The installed
supplement is labeled: complete original wikitext, exact template/math-context/
notice ranges, or archive-only with its original hash. Ranges can contain
unexpanded attribution templates. Recognized notices remain inspectable;
unrecognized notices and source-specific rights are not claimed cleared. No editorial verification,
complete rights clearance, currentness or Android acceptance is claimed.
This is an evaluation-only staging edition, not distribution-ready content.

The lean supplement policy omits recognized ordinary citation and infobox
boilerplate from installed wikitext, while retaining structured infobox fields,
unknown templates, recognized notice prose, math context and unit templates.
Exact source ranges and hashes identify retained fragments. Ambiguous markup
and unresolved source math retain whole wikitext. This policy has unknown
notice-detection false negatives and is not rights clearance.
