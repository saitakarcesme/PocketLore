# PocketLore

PocketLore is an experimental Android application for offline research with inspectable local sources. It includes CPU llama.cpp inference, retrieval, citation-linked answers, explicit abstention and extractive fallback, local model/pack import, and a small offline travel slice.

**Status: release-v5 development candidate; full-scale/product acceptance remains open.** The current APK builds reproducibly and its release checks cover a fresh offline three-edition installation, real JNI citation navigation, selection persistence, source/license inspection and rollback. Production remains pinned Qwen2.5 0.5B; selected Qwen3 4B remains host-only. Physical Android/GrapheneOS, independent unseen quality and human acceptance remain open.

See the [current candidate and raw evidence](docs/evidence/broad-candidate-release.md), [installation guide](docs/RELEASE.md) and [release gaps](docs/RELEASE_GAPS.md). The fresh emulator retains reference, science and reviewed broad editions together:1,113 distinct documents and40,891 passages. Task300's much larger first-shard browse milestone is historical and archived during this clean installation; staged bulk counts are not installed or generated-answer coverage. Bulk rights/support gates remain open.

The app requests no permissions or core Google Play Services dependency. Assets are acquired separately before offline use. The bundled travel catalog has25 central DC POIs; hours may be stale and routing is unavailable. A supported headlamp explanation and inspectable citations do not establish broad research quality.

The target is compatible Android/GrapheneOS hardware with at most 12 GB RAM and 50 GB installed assets. Host/emulator diagnostics are not phone acceptance. See [goals](docs/GOALS.md), [findings](FINDINGS.md), [comparison evidence](docs/evidence/comparison.md), [resource evidence](docs/evidence/resources.md), and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES).
