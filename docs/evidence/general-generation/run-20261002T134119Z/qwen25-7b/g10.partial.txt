Not all building insulation is exclusively thermal. In fact, the term "building insulation" can also apply to acoustic, fire, and impact insulation, in addition to thermal purposes. Often, an insulation material is chosen for its ability to perform multiple functions at once, such as thermal and acoustic insulation [[S1]].

Passive ventilation does not require a mechanical fan. It is defined as the process of supplying air to and removing air from an indoor space without using mechanical systems. This process relies on natural forces to create pressure differences, which in turn facilitate the flow of external air into the indoor space [[S2]].
