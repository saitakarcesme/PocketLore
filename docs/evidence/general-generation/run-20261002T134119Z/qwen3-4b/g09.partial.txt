Reducing heat flow through a building envelope involves using materials like insulation to minimize the transfer of thermal energy, which helps in maintaining indoor temperatures and reducing energy consumption for heating or cooling [[S1]]. In contrast, exchanging indoor and outdoor air without mechanical systems refers to passive ventilation, which relies on natural pressure differences to move air without the use of mechanical equipment [[S2]]. These two processes serve different purposes: one focuses on thermal efficiency, while the other focuses on air quality and ventilation. GAPS: none.
