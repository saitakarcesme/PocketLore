Is all building insulation exclusively thermal, and does passive ventilation require a mechanical fan? Explain both premises.

Building insulation is not exclusively thermal. While the majority of insulation in buildings is for thermal purposes, the term also applies to acoustic insulation, fire insulation, and impact insulation. Therefore, it is not accurate to say that all building insulation is exclusively thermal [[S1]].

Passive ventilation does not require a mechanical fan. Passive ventilation is the process of supplying air to and removing air from an indoor space without using mechanical systems. It relies on the flow of external air to an indoor space as a result of pressure differences arising from natural forces. Therefore, passive ventilation does not require a mechanical fan [[S2]].
