O1|P3.3,P4.1|Subject: Constitution and Constitutional Monarchy|Material time or condition, or none|The constitution defines the principles upon which the state is based and the procedure in which laws are made, while a constitutional monarch must exercise authority within the limits prescribed by an established legal framework. P4.1 emphasizes that a constitutional monarch's authority is bound by the constitution and must adhere to the legal framework, often with significant limitations on personal discretion. P3.3 highlights that the constitution establishes the principles governing the state, whereas a constitutional monarch's role is constrained by these principles and the legal framework.
