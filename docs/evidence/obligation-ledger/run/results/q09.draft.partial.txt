The United Kingdom's constitution is an example of a written constitution, even though it is not codified, as noted in P2.2.  
O1|P2.1,P2.2|codified constitution|principles are encompassed in a single comprehensive document|A codified constitution is defined as when principles are encompassed in a single comprehensive document, as stated in P2.1. The United Kingdom's constitution is not codified, as noted in P2.2, indicating that a codified constitution is not the same as a written constitution.  
O1|P2.1,P2.2|overlap|both written and codified constitution categories can overlap|The overlap between written and codified constitution categories occurs when a constitution is both written (principles are written down) and codified (principles are in a single comprehensive document), as explained in P2.1. The United Kingdom's constitution is an example of a written constitution that is not codified, as noted in P2.2.
