O1|S9,S11,S12,S13|The variation in powers of constitutional monarchs is described in the sources. S9 explains that constitutional monarchs exercise authority according to a constitution and are not alone in decision-making. S11 states that the powers of constitutional monarchs vary. S12 details that in some countries, the monarch has virtually no executive or policy-making power, while in others, they have meaningful formal powers. S13 provides specific examples of countries where the monarch has substantial discretionary powers versus those where the monarch retains significantly less, if any, personal discretion. The sources indicate that the powers of constitutional monarchs differ based on the constitutional framework and the specific country's political structure.
