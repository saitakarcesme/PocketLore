O1|P2.5,P3.1|fermentation|product|The evidence establishes that lactate is a product of fermentation, not an input. P2.5 states that lactate can be neutralized to lactic acid and used for food preservation, as a curing agent, or as a flavoring agent, indicating it is a byproduct. P3.1 confirms that fermentation can produce nearly 300 different combinations of end products, with lactate being one of them, further supporting that lactate is a result of fermentation, not a component used in the process.
