O1|P4.1,P5.1,P5.2,P6.1|Constitutional monarch|Constitutional monarchy|Constitutional monarchs do not have the same powers; their authority is determined by the constitutional framework and the specific political structure of the country. Some constitutional monarchs have significant discretionary powers, such as in Liechtenstein, Monaco, Morocco, Jordan, Kuwait, Bahrain, Thailand, and Bhutan, while others have minimal or no personal discretion, as in the United Kingdom, Netherlands, Spain, Luxembourg, Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Lesotho, Malaysia, Cambodia, and Japan. The variation in powers is due to differences in constitutional provisions and the role of the monarch within the political system.
