Pasteurization, on the other hand, uses heat to eliminate pathogens and spoilage enzymes, preserving food safety and extending shelf life. While fermentation is a metabolic process that generates specific chemicals, pasteurization is a thermal process that inactivates microorganisms. Both processes are used in food processing but serve different purposes: fermentation creates products like alcohol and lactic acid, while pasteurization ensures microbial safety through heat treatment.
