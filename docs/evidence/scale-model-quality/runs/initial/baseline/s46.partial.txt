The brightness ratio associated with a magnitude difference of one is [TeX: {\displaystyle {\sqrt[{5}]{100}}}] or about 2.512. For example, a magnitude 2.0 star is 2.512 times as bright as a magnitude 3.0 star, 6.31 times as bright as a magnitude 4.0 star, and precisely 100 times as bright as a magnitude 7.0 star.
