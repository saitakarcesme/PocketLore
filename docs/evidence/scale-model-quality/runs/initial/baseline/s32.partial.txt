[O4] [S4] Athens is one of the world's oldest cities, with its recorded history spanning over 3,400 years. This makes it a significant historical site for both democracy and learning.

[O3] [S3] Athens is one of the biggest economic centres in Southeast Europe.

[O2] [S2] Athens is the capital and largest city of Greece. A significant coastal urban area in the Mediterranean, Athens is also the capital of the Attica region and is the southernmost capital on the European mainland.
