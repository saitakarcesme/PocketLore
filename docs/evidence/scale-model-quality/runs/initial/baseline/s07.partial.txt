O1: Compost is a mixture of ingredients used as plant fertilizer and to improve soil's physical, chemical, and biological properties. It is commonly prepared by decomposing plant and food waste, recycling organic materials, and manure. The resulting mixture is rich in plant nutrients and beneficial organisms, such as bacteria, protozoa, nematodes, and fungi. Compost improves soil fertility in gardens, landscaping, horticulture, urban agriculture, and organic farming, reducing dependency on commercial chemical fertilizers.

O2: Compost is used for various purposes, including gardening, landscaping, horticulture, urban agriculture, and organic farming. It is also used in the construction of wetlands and in landfill cover. Compost is a valuable, nutrient-rich organic fertilizer and soil conditioner.
