O1: Amateur astronomers can contribute to science even when research is not their main goal by observing, studying, or imaging celestial objects in the sky using the unaided eye, binoculars, telescopes, and other gadgets. Although scientific research may not be their primary goal, some amateur astronomers contribute to citizen science by monitoring variable stars, double stars, sunspots, and occultations of stars by the Moon or asteroids. They also discover transient astronomical events, such as comets, galactic novae, or supernovae in other galaxies. By observing celestial objects, amateur astronomers can contribute to the scientific community by providing valuable data and insights into the universe.
