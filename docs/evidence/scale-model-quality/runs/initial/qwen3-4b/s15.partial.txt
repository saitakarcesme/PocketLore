O1: [S1] Grafting joins tissues of plants so that they continue their growth together. The upper part is called the scion, and the lower part is called the rootstock. The success of this joining requires that the vascular tissues grow together.  
O2: [S4] Pruning removes certain parts of a plant, such as branches, buds, or roots. It is practiced in horticulture, arboriculture, and silviculture.
