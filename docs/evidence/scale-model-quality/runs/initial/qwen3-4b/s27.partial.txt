O1: [S2] Aruba is located in the southern Caribbean Sea, 29 kilometres (18 mi) north of the Venezuelan peninsula of Paraguaná and 80 kilometres (50 mi) north-west of Curaçao. It is geologically located in South America, lying on the South American continental shelf. Aruba has a dry climate with an arid xeric landscape and relatively warm, sunny weather throughout the year.

O2: [S4] Andorra is a landlocked country on the Iberian Peninsula, in the eastern Pyrenees in southwestern Europe, bordered by France to the north and Spain to the south. Its capital and largest city is Andorra la Vella, which is the highest capital city in Europe, located at an elevation of 1,023 metres (3,356 feet) above sea level.
