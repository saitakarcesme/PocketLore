O1: [S2] Barcelona was the most important city in the Crown of Aragon and its main economic and administrative centre. It became the capital of the Principality of Catalonia after joining with the Kingdom of Aragon to form the composite monarchy of the Crown of Aragon.

O2: [S5] Classical Athens was one of the most powerful city-states in ancient Greece and a centre for Ancient Greek democracy, the arts, education and philosophy. It was highly influential throughout the Hellenistic world and the European continent, particularly in ancient Rome.
