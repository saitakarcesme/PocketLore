O1: [S4] Apparent magnitude measures the brightness of a star, astronomical object, or other celestial object, taking into account its intrinsic luminosity, distance, and any extinction caused by interstellar dust or atmosphere along the line of sight to the observer.

O2: [S1] Amateur astronomy involves observing, studying, or imaging celestial objects in the sky using the unaided eye, binoculars, telescopes, and other gadgets. It includes activities such as monitoring variable stars, double stars, sunspots, occultations of stars by the Moon or asteroids, and discovering transient astronomical events like comets, galactic novae, or supernovae in other galaxies.
