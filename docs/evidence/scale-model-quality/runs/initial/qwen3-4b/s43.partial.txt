O1: [S5] The sign and size of apparent magnitude relate to brightness such that a lower magnitude number indicates a brighter object. A difference of 1.0 in magnitude corresponds to a brightness ratio of about 2.512, meaning a magnitude 2.0 star is 2.512 times as bright as a magnitude 3.0 star. Negative magnitudes indicate very bright objects, like Venus at −4.2 or Sirius at −1.46, while positive magnitudes indicate dimmer objects, with the faintest visible stars having magnitudes around +6.5.
