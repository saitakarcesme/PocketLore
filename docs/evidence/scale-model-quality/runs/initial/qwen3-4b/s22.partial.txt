O1: [S2] A codified constitution is one in which the principles are written down into a single document or set of legal documents, while an uncodified constitution is one in which these principles are encompassed in a single comprehensive document. The United Kingdom is an example of an uncodified constitution, as its principles are found in numerous fundamental acts, court cases, and treaties.

O2: [S5] Constitutional monarchs can differ in power in that some have virtually no executive or policy-making power and are primarily symbolic heads of state, while others have meaningful formal powers such as veto power, appointment power, and the power to dissolve parliament.
