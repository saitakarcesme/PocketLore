O1: [S2] The green inputs for composting are nitrogen-rich materials such as leaves, grass, and food scraps, while the brown inputs are woody materials rich in carbon, such as stalks, paper, and wood chips.

O2: [S4] Vermicast is the end-product of the breakdown of organic matter by earthworms, and it is also called worm castings, worm humus, worm poop, worm manure, or worm faeces.
