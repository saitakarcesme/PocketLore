O1: [S4] Earthworms play a crucial role in producing vermicompost by decomposing vegetable or food waste, bedding materials, and vermicast through the process of vermicomposting. The decomposition process involves the breakdown of organic matter by earthworms, which results in the creation of vermicompost. [S5] Additionally, earthworms contribute to the breakdown of organic matter by excreting vermicast, which is the end-product of the breakdown of organic matter by earthworms. [S5] The excreta from earthworms, known as vermicast, contain reduced levels of contaminants and a higher saturation of nutrients than the organic materials before vermicomposting. [S5]
