O1: [S2] The green inputs for composting are nitrogen-rich materials such as leaves, grass, and food scraps. The brown inputs are woody materials rich in carbon, such as stalks, paper, and wood chips.

O2: [S4] Vermicast is the end-product of the decomposition process using various species of worms, usually red wigglers, white worms, and other earthworms, to break down organic matter. It is also called worm castings, worm humus, worm poop, worm manure, or worm faeces.
