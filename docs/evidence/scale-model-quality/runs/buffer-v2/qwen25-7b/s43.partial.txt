O1: [S4] The sign and size of apparent magnitude relate to brightness in a reverse logarithmic scale. The brighter an object is, the lower its magnitude number. A difference of 1.0 in magnitude corresponds to the brightness ratio of [TeX: {\displaystyle {\sqrt[{5}]{100}}}] , or about 2.512. For example, a magnitude 2.0 star is 2.512 times as bright as a magnitude 3.0 star, 6.31 times as bright as a magnitude 4.0 star, and precisely 100 times as bright as a magnitude 7.0 star.
