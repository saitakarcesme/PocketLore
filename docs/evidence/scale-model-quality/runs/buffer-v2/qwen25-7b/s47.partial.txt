O1: [S5] The scale is reverse logarithmic: the brighter an object is, the lower its magnitude number. A difference of 1.0 in magnitude corresponds to the brightness ratio of [TeX: {\displaystyle {\sqrt[{5}]{100}}}] , or about 2.512. This means a larger apparent magnitude number always means a fainter object, not a brighter one. Therefore, the statement "A larger apparent magnitude number always means a brighter object" is not supported by the evidence.
