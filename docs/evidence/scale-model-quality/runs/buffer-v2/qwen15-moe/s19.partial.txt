O1: A constitution, or established precedent, is a fundamental principle or established rule that constitutes the legal basis of a polity, organization, or other type of entity. It is written down into a single document or set of legal documents, and when it is encompassed in a single comprehensive document, it is said to embody a codified constitution. The constitution of the United Kingdom is a notable example of an uncodified constitution, written in numerous fundamental acts of a legislature, court cases, and treaties.
