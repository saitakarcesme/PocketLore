O1: Barcelona played a significant role in the Crown of Aragon during the 19th and early 20th centuries, experiencing industrialization and several workers movements. It is known for hosting the 1992 Summer Olympics and hosting world-class conferences and expositions. It is also home to two of the most prestigious universities in Spain: the University of Barcelona and Pompeu Fabra University. The headquarters of the Union for the Mediterranean are located in Barcelona.
O2: Athens, the capital and largest city of Greece, is one of the world's oldest cities, with its recorded history spanning over 3,400 years. It is known for its recorded history and its influence on Western civilization and democracy. It is also known for its maritime port, Piraeus, which is the second-busiest passenger port in Europe and the thirteenth-largest container port in the world.
