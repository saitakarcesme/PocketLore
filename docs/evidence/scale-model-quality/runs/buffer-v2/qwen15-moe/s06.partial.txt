O1: Binary search, also known as half-interval search, logarithmic search, or binary chop, is a search algorithm that finds the position of a target value within a sorted array. It runs in logarithmic time in the worst case, making [TeX: {\displaystyle O(\log n)}] comparisons, where [TeX: {\displaystyle n}] is the number of elements in the array. The measured nanosecond latency of binary search on your laptop this morning was [TeX: {\displaystyle O(\log n)}].
