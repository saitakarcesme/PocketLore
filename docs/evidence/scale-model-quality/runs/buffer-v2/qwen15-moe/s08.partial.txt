The process of vermicomposting involves the use of worms to create a mixture of decomposing vegetable or food waste, bedding materials, and vermicast. The decomposition process is aided by shredding the plant matter, adding water, and ensuring proper aeration. The end product, vermicompost, is rich in nutrients and beneficial organisms, making it an effective fertilizer and soil conditioner.
