O1|P5.1,P5.2|Pruning|material time or condition, or none|The wound-compartmentalization explanation justifies the preference for smaller cuts in pruning, as it allows woody plants to limit pathogen intrusion and decay by compartmentalizing the wound. However, it does not necessarily justify every removal of plant material, as the justification for removing specific types of plant material (e.g., diseased, non-productive, structurally unsound) is based on different criteria beyond just wound-compartmentalization.
