O1|P4.1,P4.2,P4.3,P4.4,P4.5|Andorra|location|Describing Andorra as a coastal Caribbean island would be wrong because Andorra is a landlocked country on the Iberian Peninsula in southwestern Europe, bordered by France to the north and Spain to the south. It is not located in the Caribbean Sea or any coastal area associated with it. Additionally, Andorra is the sixth-smallest state in Europe and is not part of the Caribbean islands.
