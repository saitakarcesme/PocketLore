O1|P4.1,P4.2,P4.3|Pasteurization|material time or condition, or none|Pasteurization should not be described as eliminating every bacterial spore because most bacterial spores survive the pasteurization process, as stated in P4.2. This is supported by the fact that pasteurization is designed to inactivate microorganisms and enzymes that contribute to food spoilage, but it does not eliminate all bacterial spores, as indicated in P4.2. Additionally, the process uses mild heat, which is insufficient to destroy all bacterial spores, as noted in P4.2.
