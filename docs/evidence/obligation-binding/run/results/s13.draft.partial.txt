O1|P1.1,P1.2,P1.6|Subject: Grafting|material time or condition, or none|The scion is the upper part of the combined plant, typically joined to the rootstock at the soil line, and the rootstock is the lower part, which provides the roots. In top-work grafting, the scion is joined far above the soil line, leaving an understock consisting of the lower part of the trunk and the root system.
