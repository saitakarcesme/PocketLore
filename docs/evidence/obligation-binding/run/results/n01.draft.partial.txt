O1|P4.1,P4.2,P5.1,P6.1|Vermicast|none|Vermicast is the result of the breakdown of organic matter by earthworms, not the material eaten by them. Vermicast is the end-product of the decomposition process using earthworms, while the material eaten by earthworms includes decomposing vegetable or food waste, bedding materials, and vermicast.
