O1|P1.3,P4.1,P4.2,P5.1|Subject: Compost and Vermicompost|material time or condition, or none|The organic matter processed by earthworms in vermicomposting is decomposed plant and food waste, bedding materials, and vermicast, resulting in excreta that are rich in plant nutrients and beneficial organisms, such as bacteria, protozoa, nematodes, and fungi. References: P1.3 describes the composition of compost, P4.1 defines vermicompost as the product of decomposition using earthworms, P4.2 explains the process of vermicomposting, and P5.1 details the end-product of vermicomposting, which is vermicast.
