[S1] Volcanoes are openings, or vents, where lava, tephra, and steam erupt onto the Earth's surface.
[S2] Magma can be erupted in a variety of ways, including as fluid lava flows, dense clouds of rock shards (tephra), and gas.
[S2] Larger fragments fall back around the vent, and clouds of tephra may move down the slope of the volcano under the force of gravity.
[S2] Ash, tiny pieces of tephra the thickness of a strand of hair, may be carried by the wind only to fall to the ground many miles away.
