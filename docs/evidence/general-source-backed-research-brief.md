# Task 225: evidence-first research brief

## Declaration before implementation

Task 224's preserved final checkpoint `79d2b94` records four failed designs: lossy finite extraction, whole-dependency matching, a formula restatement without explanation, and an obligation ledger whose untrusted accounts invented comparisons and chronology. Its final 24-case run had four useful builder-reviewed drafts but seven unsupported candidates. No old inference will be repeated.

This design removes free factual prose and planner accounts from the brief path. A general ranker selects bounded **whole source passages**, retaining subject context, conditions, dates, rights and typed edition-aware links. The renderer emits explicit quotation blocks and procedural coverage warnings only. It never turns quotation or rank into entailment or successful generation. Question text is visibly labeled as the user's question, not asserted as fact. Source inspection evaluates relevance, complete coverage and usefulness independently of byte integrity. Incomplete retrieval remains visibly unresolved; no model silently fills gaps.

The frozen 24 public development questions and exact licensed source bytes are in `tools/evaluation/research-brief`. Seven subject areas extend beyond the old finite relation families: molecular genetics, chromosome packaging, tectonics, volcanology, geomagnetism, space weather and wilderness preparedness. They include paraphrases, cross-document comparisons, false-premise corrections, conditions and three absent controls. All available passages also serve as distractors. They are selected from existing reviewed science/reference editions, not new corpus coverage or held-out evaluation. Some excerpts were available to earlier development work; contamination risk remains.

Fixed implementation policy: existing BM25 ranking, at most four whole passages and 8,192 UTF-16 text units; no sentence truncation, fact-specific transformations, topic/entity/ID allowlists, inference, seed or threshold search. A missing requested part is not certified by ranking. Quotes are research material, never auto-certified complete answers. Full provenance is visible; unreviewed or missing rights metadata cannot receive a reviewed-brief designation. Cancellation discards the whole pending result.

The quality gate will report builder source assessments separately from independent review. Twelve complete useful briefs and zero unsupported eligible outputs are required; pending independent source review cannot be represented as a passing quality gate. Production remains the pinned Qwen2.5 0.5B; Qwen3 4B SHA `7485fe6f11af29433bc51cab58009521f205840f5b4ae3a32fa7f92e8534fdf5` remains an experimental host candidate and is not used to generate quotes. Android limits and model pins stay unchanged.

Discriminating controls: exact UTF-16 quote offsets including non-BMP text; math/brackets remain text; changed source/manifest/run artifacts; missing sources/rights; cancellation; no partial truncation of subjects or conditions; historical unsupported drafts must never enter the quoted renderer; mixed editions cannot alias. All final outputs and negative results will be retained.
