# Task500 strategy change: evidence review is an explicit boundary

## Preserved failures

Checkpoint2c9f282 records three unsuccessful product attempts. General chat drafts and application wiring did not supply a qualified semantic verifier. The separate7B whole-answer classifier retained four useful educational drafts but approved five unsupported drafts and both unavailable controls. Six positive/negative probes passing did not establish support. Selected4B Android execution and current complete distribution measurements are also absent. Recovery preserves these immutable runs; this task will not regenerate their questions, tune verifier prompts/thresholds or count another model's approval as evidence.

## Materially different design

Replace the opaque yes/no support interface with a fail-closed, review-bound publication contract. An independently inspected answer must carry a complete review record binding the exact question, full unmodified draft, ordered source identities/text/provenance, explicit question obligations, and every factual claim's UTF16 span to exact source spans. The record distinguishes support, completeness and absence; it is invalidated by any text, scope, source, question or revision change. Every non-whitespace output character must belong to an inspected claim or an explicitly reviewed nonfactual region. Unsupported tails cannot be deleted or left unreviewed. Review records are not supplied by the generator or accepted from model scores.

This is a different trust boundary, not another automatic entailment mechanism: publication requires an independent review authority which the ordinary offline app currently does not possess. No reviewer is fabricated, no finite question lookup is used at runtime, and user/model-authored records cannot qualify themselves. A host test authority may exercise the structural contract but is explicitly NOT a production verifier. Existing independently inspected positive and negative drafts remain development evidence, not reusable runtime answer templates. General source briefs remain available and distinctly extractive. The strategy can implement necessary whole-output evidence binding; it cannot honestly claim unrestricted supported generation is solved without a qualified review source.

## Discriminating checks frozen before implementation

Test exact successful binding with a visibly labeled constructed transport fixture; reject changed question, draft, source text, URL, date, license, collection identity and ordering; reject missing or overlapping factual coverage, whitespace-only claims, unsupported tails, unsupported/incomplete judgments, absent requests, shifted UTF16 surrogate boundaries, missing source spans, cancellation and an untrusted authority. All tests are contract tests, not factual entailment demonstrations. Replay the preserved negative experiment only through its immutable evidence checker, not new inference. Build the actual Android-compatible contract with two Gradle workers/2GiB heap.

The required product checker must remain failing if semantic authority, selected-model Android execution or current complete distribution measurements are absent. No simulator, certificate hash, review stub or quoted brief may turn that failure into generated quality. Record the narrower implementation result and exact unresolved dependency. Physical12GB/GrapheneOS, complete45/50GB inventory, independent unseen quality and human acceptance remain open. No new models, services, device resets or orchestration changes are part of this bounded strategy.
