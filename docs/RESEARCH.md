## Engineering consequences

Measure time to a correct usable completed answer, not only first token. Evaluate semantic source support rather than word overlap. Preserve city and dietary distinctions for travel; show coverage, data age and unknowable current opening status. Account for all installation and temporary files. Compare exact released versions under common conditions. Broad Wikipedia, RAG, local inference and POI lists are entry capabilities, not superiority evidence.

The separate read-only atlas attempt ended with an incomplete prototype. Its recorded failures included unrelated retrieval, qualifier/citation errors and withholding all drafts despite successful runtime plumbing. PocketLore must distinguish a functioning runtime from useful research and avoid spending the whole effort on orchestration or formal quote gates. Its arbitrary exhausted task cap will not be reproduced.
